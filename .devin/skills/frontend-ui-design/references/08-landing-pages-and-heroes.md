# 08 — Landing Pages and Hero Systems

## Core hero rule
Choose the hero composition based on the product story, not on current trends.

## Hero Collection patterns

### Centered editorial hero
Best for broad positioning, travel, lifestyle, editorial, discovery.
Characteristics:
- centered headline
- quiet navigation
- supporting cards or content beneath
- playful or expressive imagery

### Product-screenshot-led SaaS hero
Best for workflow/productivity tools.
Characteristics:
- concise headline
- product UI as proof
- soft glow/gradient may support atmosphere
- screenshot should explain more than long copy

### Split-screen conversion hero
Best for direct product conversion.
Characteristics:
- visual/image on one side
- message + CTA hierarchy on the other
- strong scan path
- useful for AI, education, B2B SaaS

### Image-led emotional hero
Best for lifestyle, nonprofit, education, service products.
Characteristics:
- one dominant human or domain image
- reduced surrounding chrome
- emotion carries interest while copy stays concise

### Multi-device/product ecosystem hero
Best for productivity or cross-device products.
Characteristics:
- multiple devices/screens arranged with depth
- centered positioning statement
- show ecosystem capability visually

### Premium product/fintech hero
Best for financial or premium consumer products.
Characteristics:
- spacious light canvas
- restrained typography
- controlled warm accent
- large product photography

## AI language-learning reference
Learned patterns:
- clean white base + soft lavender accent
- one strategic phrase highlighted in the headline
- realistic human video-call visual for warmth
- filled primary CTA + outlined secondary CTA
- compact language selectors with recognizable flags
- rounded supporting modules

Variant lesson:
- centered version supports more explanation/modules
- split version is faster to scan and more conversion-oriented

## AI logistics reference
Learned patterns:
- very light cool canvas
- blue accent
- small product-category pill above hero
- centered two-line headline with only one phrase colored
- narrow supporting copy
- quiet secondary CTA + stronger demo CTA
- huge domain image directly representing logistics
- strong symmetry and central axis
- minimal cards/effects because the image already provides richness

## Clean minimal SaaS reference
Learned patterns:
- large whitespace
- restrained blue accent
- simple nav
- strong headline-first hero
- short copy
- one primary CTA and quieter secondary CTA
- subtle gradients/glows only when needed
- trust logos kept quiet
- product calculator/screens as proof
- stronger final CTA/footer section

Minimal does not mean empty. It means disciplined hierarchy, spacing, and selective emphasis.

## Product-led fintech reference
Patterns:
- white base with pastel blue/lilac/mint panels
- occasional dark charcoal sections for visual reset
- product/device mockups as primary proof
- modular feature cards with varied composition but consistent alignment
- quiet partner logos
- pricing can use a dark section for focus
- FAQ/blog remain visually lighter
- dark footer closes the experience

## Nonprofit / impact reference
Patterns:
- pale mint canvas
- deep forest green anchor sections
- bright lime accent used sparingly
- oversized human-centered headline
- dual CTA hierarchy
- image triptych for storytelling
- trust logos early
- dark story section combines narrative, team photography, impact metrics
- stacked metric cards communicate scale quickly

Principle: let people and mission carry emotion; let UI supply structure and credibility.

## Long-page rhythm
A long page should not feel visually flat. Use pacing:
- restrained hero
- proof/trust
- product or feature explanation
- visual reset (light/dark or composition change)
- testimonials / credibility
- decision section (pricing, CTA)
- FAQ / secondary information
- strong closing/footer

Do not make every section equally loud.

## CTA hierarchy
A common high-quality pattern:
- primary: filled, strongest contrast
- secondary: outlined or quiet

## Headline emphasis
Highlight one strategic phrase rather than coloring the entire heading.

## Rule
> One dominant message + one dominant visual is usually stronger than several competing ideas in the hero.
