# Workflow: Government data provenance
**When:** Municipal content, demographic figures, officers, official forms or publications.

- Source all Talibon data from authorized municipal documents/approved CMS records; capture source, owner, verification date and publication status.
- Existing baseline includes legacy seed/migration data that may be placeholders. Never promote them as real data or overwrite verified records.
- Unknown details: leave blank or label “Information not yet available,” subject to content owner approval.
- For document publishing, verify approval, audience visibility, version/date and accessible description; don't manufacture official seal endorsements or fees.
- Apply privacy review for personal data and audit edits to official content.
- Require municipal content-owner signoff before public release.
