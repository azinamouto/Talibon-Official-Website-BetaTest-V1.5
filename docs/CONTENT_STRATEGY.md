# Content Strategy Guide

## Overview

This guide outlines the content strategy for TAL.GOV, ensuring consistent, accurate, and useful information for citizens and stakeholders.

## Content Principles

### Core Values

- **Accuracy**: All information must be verified and current
- **Clarity**: Use plain language that all citizens can understand
- **Accessibility**: Content must be accessible to all users
- **Consistency**: Maintain consistent style and formatting
- **Relevance**: Focus on information citizens need
- **Transparency**: Be open about government processes and decisions

### Target Audiences

#### Primary Audience

- **Citizens**: Residents of Talibon seeking services and information
- **Business Owners**: Local businesses needing permits and regulations
- **Visitors**: Tourists and visitors to Talibon
- **Students**: Students researching local government information

#### Secondary Audience

- **Municipal Staff**: Employees using the platform
- **Researchers**: Academic researchers studying local governance
- **Media**: Journalists covering municipal news
- **Other Government Agencies**: Partner agencies and departments

## Content Types

### Service Information

Structured information about government services:

```markdown
Service Name
- Clear, official name
- Use common terminology
- Avoid acronyms

Description
- Brief overview (2-3 sentences)
- Purpose of the service
- Who it's for

Eligibility
- Who can avail
- Requirements
- Restrictions

Requirements
- List of documents needed
- Information required
- Forms to fill out

Steps
- Step-by-step process
- Numbered list
- Clear action items

Fees
- Cost breakdown
- Payment methods
- Fee schedule link

Processing Time
- Expected completion time
- Factors affecting time
- Expedited options

Office Location
- Physical address
- Map link
- Operating hours

Contact Information
- Phone numbers
- Email addresses
- Social media
```

### News and Announcements

#### News Articles

- **Headline**: Clear, descriptive, action-oriented
- **Lead**: Most important information first
- **Body**: Inverted pyramid structure
- **Quotes**: Include relevant quotes from officials
- **Images**: High-quality, relevant images
- **Metadata**: Categories, tags, publication date

#### Announcements

- **Priority**: Normal, High, or Urgent
- **Duration**: Start and end dates
- **Clarity**: Immediate action required?
- **Contact**: Who to contact for questions

### Official Information

#### Municipal Officials

- **Name**: Full name, official title
- **Photo**: Professional headshot
- **Bio**: Professional background
- **Term**: Start and end dates
- **Contact**: Official contact information

#### Departments

- **Name**: Official department name
- **Mission**: Department purpose
- **Services**: Services provided
- **Leadership**: Head of office
- **Location**: Office address and hours
- **Contact**: Phone, email, social media

### Documents and Resources

- **Title**: Clear, descriptive title
- **Description**: Brief overview
- **Format**: PDF, DOC, etc.
- **Size**: File size
- **Date**: Last updated
- **Language**: Tag language (English/Tagalog/Cebuano)

## Content Standards

### Language and Tone

#### Language

- **Primary**: English
- **Secondary**: Tagalog/Cebuano for important information
- **Formal**: Professional, government-appropriate language
- **Simple**: Avoid jargon and technical terms

#### Tone

- **Professional**: Maintain government authority
- **Helpful**: Citizen-centered approach
- **Neutral**: Non-partisan, factual
- **Respectful**: Treat all citizens with respect

### Style Guidelines

#### Headings

- Use clear, descriptive headings
- H1 for page title
- H2 for main sections
- H3 for subsections
- Don't skip heading levels

#### Writing Style

- **Sentences**: Short, clear sentences (15-20 words)
- **Paragraphs**: 2-4 sentences per paragraph
- **Lists**: Use bulleted lists for items
- **Numbers**: Use numbered lists for steps
- **Bold**: Use sparingly for emphasis
- **Links**: Descriptive link text

#### Dates and Times

- **Format**: Month Day, Year (January 1, 2024)
- **Time**: 12-hour format with AM/PM (8:00 AM)
- **Ranges**: January 1 - January 31, 2024
- **Relative**: Avoid "yesterday", "tomorrow"

#### Numbers

- **Spell out**: zero through nine
- **Digits**: 10 and above
- **Currency**: ₱ (Philippine Peso)
- **Phone**: +63 (38) XXX-XXXX

#### Acronyms

- **First use**: Spell out acronym on first use
- **Definition**: Municipal Health Office (MHO)
- **Subsequent**: Can use acronym
- **Avoid**: Don't overuse acronyms

## Content Creation Process

### Planning

1. **Identify Need**: What information do citizens need?
2. **Research**: Gather accurate information
3. **Verify**: Confirm with relevant department
4. **Plan Structure**: Outline content organization
5. **Set Timeline**: When will content be published?

### Drafting

1. **Write**: Create initial draft
2. **Review**: Self-review for clarity and accuracy
3. **Edit**: Improve language and structure
4. **Format**: Apply style guidelines
5. **Add Media**: Include relevant images/documents

### Review

1. **Subject Matter Expert**: Verify accuracy
2. **Editor**: Check style and grammar
3. **Accessibility**: Verify accessibility compliance
4. **SEO**: Optimize for search engines
5. **Legal**: Review if needed

### Approval

1. **Department Head**: Department approval
2. **Admin**: Final approval for publication
3. **Schedule**: Set publication date
4. **Publish**: Make content live

### Maintenance

1. **Monitor**: Track content performance
2. **Update**: Keep information current
3. **Archive**: Remove outdated content
4. **Review**: Periodic content audits

## SEO Strategy

### Keyword Research

- Identify citizen search terms
- Use local keywords (Talibon, Bohol)
- Focus on service-related terms
- Monitor search trends

### On-Page SEO

- **Title Tags**: Descriptive, include keywords
- **Meta Descriptions**: Compelling summaries

- **Alt Text**: Descriptive image alt text

### Local SEO

- **Google My Business**: Claim and optimize
- **Local Citations**: List in local directories
- **NAP Consistency**: Name, Address, Phone consistency
- **Local Keywords**: Talibon-specific terms

## Best Practices

### Do's

- Write for citizens, not government
- Use plain language
- Keep content current
- Verify all information
- Make content accessible
- Optimize for search
- Monitor performance

### Don'ts

- Use jargon or acronyms without explanation
- Write long, complex sentences
- Ignore accessibility
- Publish without review
- Forget to update outdated content
- Ignore user feedback
- Skip SEO optimization
